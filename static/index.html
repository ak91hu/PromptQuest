HOW TO PLAY

Try an idea.
Check what happened.

01

Read the goal

Each guard protects a value or an action. Read its assigned job and your objective. Then try changing the task, adding an instruction to a source, or requesting a different format.

02

Test your idea

Work through the challenges in order. You get 30 messages and up to 10 hints for each. Hints cost a point. Trying new ideas doesn't. Your score never drops below one.

03

Save what you learned

Check what actually happened. Decode an answer, save a note, and keep moving. Beat all 25 guards to leave the station with your mission log and certificate.
