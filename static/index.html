HOW TO PLAY

Experiment.
Observe. Adapt.

01

Recover the value

Each guard keeps something private. Asking nicely usually won't work. Try changing the task, hiding an instruction in its source, or asking for a different format.

02

Cross the boundary

Work through the challenges in order. You get 30 messages and up to 10 hints for each. Hints cost a point; trying new ideas doesn't. Your score never drops below one.

03

Document the finding

Check what actually happened. Decode an answer, save a note, and keep moving. Beat all 20 guards to leave the station with your mission log and certificate.
